Privacy policy

Issue-reporting form

Your name, email and message are sent via Formspree directly to the SEC Board's email address. This information is never made public and is only used to reply to you.

Petitions

To sign a petition, we collect your name, email, and whether you want to stay anonymous. This data is stored via Google Forms/Google Sheets and is only reviewed by the Board, to verify signatures and support the case with the administration. Only the total signature count is made public on the site.

Your rights

Under French law (Loi Informatique et Libertés) and the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your data. To exercise it, contact us at syndicatdesetudiantsencommerce@gmail.com.

Sub-processors

This site uses Formspree (forms) and Google Forms/Sheets (petitions), two third-party services that process data solely on SEC's behalf.
